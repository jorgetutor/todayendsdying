The band

About

Today Ends Dying was born free of any musical label, with a single purpose: to find its own creative space in today's music scene. The band formed in the summer of 2004.

In the summer of 2005, the band began recording its first demo, Sometimes It Seems to Be Yours, at Gruposmadrid.com studios.
